## Overall Impression

The page is disciplined and technically clean — no console errors, clean heading order, a real focus-ring and skip-link system, deliberate avoidance of fabricated social proof. The gap isn't execution quality, it's that the visual system reads as generic dev-studio-template while the copy layer is doing real, specific work the visuals don't match. The single biggest opportunity: the highest-stakes moment on the page (deciding to ask for a quote) has zero trust reinforcement beyond self-description.

## What's Working

- **HeroVisual's animated wave-in-browser-chrome** is a literal, on-brand centerpiece showing the real domain — rare for a template-driven landing page to earn its hero visual this directly.
- **Process.jsx's per-step `Resultado:` field** converts a generic 5-step waterfall into concrete deliverables ("link de preview atualizado a cada entrega") — implicit proof without fake testimonials.
- **Accessibility discipline**: one global `:focus-visible` ring system, correct `aria-expanded`/`aria-controls` wiring on the mobile nav, Esc + outside-click to close, and a 3-second failsafe in `useReveal.js` so a broken observer never permanently hides content.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] No trust signal before the CTA.** Why it matters: asking a stranger for a quote is the single highest-stakes moment on the page, and right now it's backed only by self-description — no external proof exists to reinforce it. Fix: since real testimonials/case studies don't exist (confirmed — do not fabricate them), add a concrete de-risking mechanic instead: reinforce the "resposta por escrito" commitment closer to the CTA, add a "sem compromisso" framing, or point to the site itself as a live capability demo. Suggested command: `$impeccable clarify`

**[P1] Services (5 cards) and Features (5 items) both exceed the ≤4 chunking guideline.** Why it matters: forces the visitor to self-diagnose across 5 undifferentiated peer options right before the ask, adding cognitive load exactly where the page most wants a decision. Fix: tier into 3 core + 2 secondary, or fold a lower-priority item (e.g. "Automação com IA") into an existing card's description rather than a peer card. Suggested command: `$impeccable distill`

**[P2] Header nav shows 5 simultaneous targets on every screen** (4 section links + the Contato pill). Why it matters: competes with the single primary action the entire page exists to drive. Fix: demote one link into a sub-section, or visually separate nav links from the CTA so the CTA reads as singular. Suggested command: `$impeccable quieter`

**[P2] On mobile, the one distinctive hero visual sits below a text wall.** Why it matters: single-column stacking in the hero puts the commitments list before HeroVisual, so a scanning mobile visitor may scroll past the most differentiated asset on the page before seeing it. Fix: reorder or compress the commitments list so the visual surfaces sooner on narrow viewports. Suggested command: `$impeccable layout`

**[P3] About/Services/Process/Features repeat an identical eyebrow+h2+lead header pattern.** Why it matters: monotonous rhythm across a long scroll despite genuinely different content types (steps vs. cards vs. list) — a missed chance to signal "we sweat details" through the layout itself, not just the copy. Fix: vary the layout treatment per section type. Suggested command: `$impeccable polish`
